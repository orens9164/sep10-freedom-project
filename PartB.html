Ideas For Future Technology

Idea#1: My first idea is an AI controlled saw machine. The vehicle will have a scanner on it that will help it determine if there are any trees nearby. The front of the machine will have saws so that it can cut down the trees. Once the trees are cut down, they fall on top of the back of the vehicle. On the back of the truck, ther will be clamps that are enabled by pressure, so when the log lands and presses down against the truck bed, it will be held there and kept still as the truck is moving. Once it senses a certain amount of weight, or all the clamps are taken up, it wil drive to a designated area and the bed will tilt, dumping the logs in a convenient area for workers to collect.

Idea#2: My second idea is a human controlled catching mechanism. One of the biggest problems when it comes to lumber is the possibility of a tree falling on someone. This machine will allow somebody to catch the tree before it falls. They can then lower it to the ground carefully. Once its lowered, they can activate a part of the mechanism that will make gears turn inside the catcher, pushing it up the tree and stripping off the branches.
